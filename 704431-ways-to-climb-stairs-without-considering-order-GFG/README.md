# [Ways to Climb Stairs Without Considering Order](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/count-ways-to-nth-stairorder-does-not-matter5639/1)
## Medium
There are n stairs, and a person standing at the bottom wants to reach the top. The person can climb either 1 stair or 2 stairs at a time. Count the number of ways, the person can reach the top, Order does not matte which means different permutations of same set of steps are considered same. For example, for n = 4,&nbsp; {1 2 1}, {2 1 1} and 1 1 2} are considered same.Examples :Input: n = 4
Output: 3
Explanation: Three ways to reach at 4th stair. They are {1, 1, 1, 1}, {1, 1, 2}, {2, 2}.
Input: n = 5
Output: 3
Explanation: Three ways to reach at 5th stair. They are {1, 1, 1, 1, 1}, {1, 1, 2, 1} and {1, 2, 2}.
